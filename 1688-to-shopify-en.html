Accio Work 3-Prompt SOP

Accio Work runs in local sandbox at ~/.accio/, processes only user-authorized images/text, no cloud transmission of store API keys. Paste these prompts into your Agent directly.

1

Step 1 · Image Search & Compare (Filter Candidates)

Search 1688 for similar products using this image. Filter candidates with rating ≥ 4.6, sales ≥ 500, supporting dropshipping. Output Top 10 table (with store link / unit price / MOQ)

Agent uses the "find similar products" capability launched in v0.16.0, returning a side-drawer comparison list.

2

Step 2 · Factory Verification (Factory vs Trading Co.)

Verify public business registry for these 10 suppliers: legal person's related companies, whether business scope includes manufacturing, insured employee count. Output "Factory / Trading / Unclear" three-tier judgment with rationale

Legal persons linked to many trading companies + business scope missing "manufacturing" are typically trading companies in disguise. Measured: trading-company hit rate drops from 40% to 8%.

3

Step 3 · English Copy Rewrite (Listing)

Rewrite this 1688 Chinese description into Shopify product page English copy: H1 title ≤ 70 chars, 5 Bullet Points highlighting core benefits, Description paragraph 300 words with long-tail keywords, in authentic American English

For Amazon, swap "Shopify" with "Amazon" and add "include 5 SEO backend keywords". For Shopee, halve the length and add SEA localization hints.

Compliance note (Anti-Ban 2.3): All prompts run inside local ~/.accio/ sandbox, only reading user-authorized images/text. Business registry data comes from public sources — not official verification. For multi-store setups, use Accio Work's independent workspace isolation — never share login state.

Shopify / Amazon / Shopee — Platform-Specific Checklist

Shopify
  • H1 ≤ 70 chars with long-tail keywords
  • 5 Bullets · benefit-focused not spec-focused
  • Description 300+ words · SEO paragraph
  • Use Metafield for long-form story
Amazon
  • Title ≤ 200 chars · Brand + Keyword + Variant
  • 5 Bullets · CAPITALIZE opening word
  • 5 backend keywords (invisible but indexed)
  • A+ page with EBC templates
Shopee
  • Title 100 chars · category word first
  • Description 200 words · include SEA local slang (ID/VN/TH)
  • 10 main images · vertical · emphasize price anchor
  • SEA → mark COD support explicitly
